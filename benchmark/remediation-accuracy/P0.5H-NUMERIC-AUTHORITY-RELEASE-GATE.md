**Do not automatically deploy. Do not enable numeric authority in production as part of this change.**

| ID | Case | Expected |

Additional: fresh “preserves 61% of minerals” recovers without a phrase-specific rule; `off` does not invoke the authority service; timeout fail-open.

## Production parity when off

Default `AAIRP_SEMANTIC_NUMERIC_AUTHORITY` is `off`. Pipeline skips the authority service. Decision fusion is Rule + Playbook + stub Open Risk only.

## Isolation

- General Semantic Shadow: independent `AAIRP_SEMANTIC_SHADOW_MODE`, still non-authoritative.
- Open Risk: still stub unless a separate change sets live (not this release).
- Candidate Reading / rewrite: not modified.
